These blog posts are written in MDX files, as noted on [MDXJS.com](https://mdxjs.com):

"MDX lets you use JSX in your markdown content. You can import components, such as interactive charts or alerts, and embed them within your content. This makes writing long-form content with components a blast. "

This is all on top of everything that markdown already provides, here is the original cheatsheet from the tailwind starter [blog](./blog/guide-to-using-images-in-nextjs/)

And [here](./blog/github-markdown-guide/) is a guide to using images.
